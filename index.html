Why Home237

Everything you need to rent with confidence

Built for the way Cameroon rents — trusted, mobile-first and secure.

Verified listings

Every property is reviewed and approved by our team before it goes live — no scams, no ghost listings.

Smart Escrow viewings

Pay viewing fees safely. Funds are released only when you scan the agent's QR code at the property.

Mobile Money payments

Pay rent and fees with MTN MoMo or Orange Money. Fast, familiar and secure — no bank card needed.

Digital leases

Sign a legally-worded tenancy agreement online. Both parties get a copy by email instantly.

AI rental assistant

Our bilingual "Virtual Démarcheur" answers questions and finds you homes in English or French, 24/7.

Boost & go Premium
